# Pir2pir

Pir2pir helps School 21 participants find each other for mutual reviews of study projects.

- Telegram bot: [@pir2pirbot](https://t.me/pir2pirbot)
- Personal data requests: legal@pir2pir.ru

## How it works

1. Open the bot and enter your School 21 login.
2. Confirm you own the account with the code sent to your student email.
3. Find a participant by login — in the bot, or in any chat via `@pir2pirbot login`.

not act on its behalf.
