## 2. Data subject

The subject is a natural person who has connected their School 21 account to Pir2Pir and confirmed
ownership of it with a code sent to their student email at `{login}@student.21-school.ru`.

Confirming that mailbox is how the subject is identified for the purposes of this Consent.

## 3. Personal data processed

The Operator processes the data listed below and requests no other categories.

### 3.1. Identification data

- School 21 account login;
- student email address at `{login}@student.21-school.ru`;
- first and last name as present in the Telegram profile;
- Telegram numeric user id (`telegram_user_id`);
- Telegram username (`@username`), if set.

### 3.2. Study profile, retrieved from the School 21 public API

- campus, wave and education form;
- level, experience, internal points and review points;
- list of projects and their statuses.

### 3.3. Image

- Telegram profile photo, unless the subject declines its use as an avatar.

### 3.4. Technical data

- internal Pir2Pir account identifiers;
- service request logs required for diagnostics and security.

The Operator does **not** process special categories of personal data (Article 10) or biometric
personal data (Article 11).

## 4. Purposes of processing

Consent is granted solely for the following purposes:

1. confirming ownership of a School 21 account and creating a Pir2Pir account;
2. finding and matching participants for mutual project reviews;
3. displaying the participant card to other verified participants searching by login;
4. delivering service notifications via Telegram;
5. handling support requests;
6. complying with the legislation of the Russian Federation.

Processing for any other purpose — in particular advertising, promotion, or transfer to recruiting or
other commercial services — is **not covered by this Consent** and requires separate consent.

## 5. Processing actions

Collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, transfer
(provision, access) within the scope of section 6, anonymisation, blocking, deletion, destruction.

Processing is both automated and non-automated. The Operator makes no decisions producing legal
effects for the subject solely on the basis of automated processing.

## 6. Disclosure to other participants

Verified participants can find a participant card by School 21 login. The card shows: login, name,
level and experience, wave, and the Telegram username and profile photo where set.

Disclosing the Telegram username and profile photo to other participants is a **direct subject of this
Consent**: without it, participant search does not work.

## 7. Validity and withdrawal

This Consent is valid from the moment it is granted until the purposes are achieved or it is
withdrawn.

Withdrawal is performed by:

- the account deletion command in the Telegram bot [@pir2pirbot](https://t.me/pir2pirbot); or
- a written request to legal@pir2pir.ru.

After withdrawal the Operator stops processing and destroys the personal data within 30 days, except
data whose retention is required by law. Withdrawal makes the service unusable, since processing the
listed data is necessary for it to function.

## 8. Rights of the data subject

The subject may obtain information about the processing of their data, require its correction,
blocking or destruction, withdraw this Consent, and appeal the Operator's actions to Roskomnadzor or
in court.

The procedure is described in the [Privacy Policy](./privacy_policy.md).

## 9. Confirmation

By granting this Consent the subject confirms that they:

- act freely, of their own will and in their own interest;
- have read the purposes, data list and processing actions above;
- have been informed of the withdrawal procedure and its consequences;
- are entitled to provide the data listed.

Consent is granted in a form allowing the fact of its receipt to be confirmed: by pressing the
confirmation button in the Telegram bot. The Operator records the date and time.
