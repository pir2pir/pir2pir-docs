## 2. Data subject

The subject is a natural person who has connected their School 21 account to Pir2Pir and confirmed
ownership of it with a code sent to their student email at `{login}@student.21-school.ru`.

Confirming that mailbox is how the subject is identified for the purposes of this Consent.

## 3. Personal data processed

The Operator processes the data listed below and requests no other categories.

### 3.1. Identification data

- School 21 account login;
- student email address at `{login}@student.21-school.ru`;
- first and last name as present in the Telegram profile;
- Telegram numeric user id (`telegram_user_id`);
- Telegram username (`@username`), if set.

### 3.2. Study profile, retrieved from the School 21 public API

- campus, wave and education form;
- level, experience, internal points and review points;
- list of projects and their statuses.

### 3.3. Image

- Telegram profile photo, unless the subject declines its use as an avatar.

### 3.4. Chat content

- the text of messages participants exchange in a chat after both agree to a review;
- files attached to those messages (images, documents, archives);
- a short excerpt of a message (up to 200 characters) included in a notification delivered to the web
  application; no such excerpt leaves the Operator's infrastructure.

Chat content is kept for a limited time: once a conversation ends, message text and attachments are
deleted and only the fact that a review took place is retained. The retention period is stated in the
Privacy Policy.

### 3.5. Technical data

- internal Pir2Pir account identifiers;
- service request logs required for diagnostics and security.

The Operator does **not** process special categories of personal data (Article 10) or biometric
personal data (Article 11).

## 4. Purposes of processing

Consent is granted solely for the following purposes:

1. confirming ownership of a School 21 account and creating a Pir2Pir account;
2. finding and matching participants for mutual project reviews;
3. sending a request to talk to a specific participant identified by their exact School 21 login;
4. enabling correspondence between participants who have agreed to a review or to talk;
5. delivering service notifications to connected channels (web application, Telegram bot);
6. sending a single invitation to a School 21 student who is not registered with the service, at a
   participant's request — within the scope and on the terms of section 6.2;
7. handling support requests;
8. complying with the legislation of the Russian Federation.

Processing for any other purpose — in particular advertising, promotion, or transfer to recruiting or
other commercial services — is **not covered by this Consent** and requires separate consent.

## 5. Processing actions

Collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, transfer
(provision, access) within the scope of section 6, anonymisation, blocking, deletion, destruction.

Processing is both automated and non-automated. The Operator makes no decisions producing legal
effects for the subject solely on the basis of automated processing.

## 6. Disclosure to other participants

The service keeps no directory of participants and offers no search for people. Reaching a specific
participant requires knowing their exact School 21 login in advance; the service offers no
suggestions and no matching on part of a login.

Identity is disclosed only at the moment of agreement. While a search for a reviewer is running, a
candidate is told the project and the campus, not who is asking. Once both have agreed, participants
see login, name, level and experience, wave, and the profile photo where set.

**The Operator does not disclose one participant's messenger accounts, email address or other contact
details to another participant.** If participants choose to continue elsewhere, they exchange such
details themselves, inside the chat, and it is not the Operator who disclosed them.

### 6.1. Chat content

Messages in a chat are available only to its two participants. The Operator does not make them
available to other users of the service.

**Chat content does not leave the Operator's infrastructure.** A new-message notification is
delivered only to the web application. A messenger is told only that a reviewer was found or that a
request to talk was received; no excerpt, no full text and no attachments are sent to a messenger.

### 6.2. Contacting a student who is not registered with the service

Where a login given by a participant belongs to a School 21 student who is not registered with the
service, the participant may send them **one short invitation**. The Operator delivers it in its own
name, to the email address derived from the login under School 21's convention and/or to School 21's
internal chat.

The invitation contains: the sender's login, the text they wrote, a brief description of the service
and an opt-out link. No other information about the sender or the recipient is transmitted, and **the
sender is not given the recipient's address** — they are shown only a masked hint of the form
`g*****@…`.

Processing is limited as follows:

- no more than one invitation to one recipient from one sender in any 30 days;
- no more than three invitations to one recipient from all senders in any 30 days;
- no more than ten invitations per day from one sender.

**Opting out.** Every invitation carries a link that takes effect immediately, permanently and across
all channels; it requires no account and no reason. After an opt-out the Operator sends nothing
further to that login. The opt-out is retained even if the recipient later registers with the
service.

The recipient of an invitation is not a participant and has not given this Consent. The basis for
processing their login and the address derived from it is the sending of a single message at a
participant's initiative; the message itself serves as notice to the recipient of what is processed
about them, where it came from and how to stop it. No account is created for such a recipient.

## 7. Validity and withdrawal

This Consent is valid from the moment it is granted until the purposes are achieved or it is
withdrawn.

Withdrawal is performed by:

- the account deletion command in the Telegram bot [@pir2pirbot](https://t.me/pir2pirbot); or
- a written request to legal@pir2pir.ru.

After withdrawal the Operator stops processing and destroys the personal data within 30 days, except
data whose retention is required by law. Withdrawal makes the service unusable, since processing the
listed data is necessary for it to function.

## 8. Rights of the data subject

The subject may obtain information about the processing of their data, require its correction,
blocking or destruction, withdraw this Consent, and appeal the Operator's actions to Roskomnadzor or
in court.

The procedure is described in the [Privacy Policy](./privacy_policy.md).
